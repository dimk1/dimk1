## Dimitrios Schoinianakis

Cryptography and security researcher — Distinguished Member of Technical Staff (DMTS), Nokia Bell Labs.
PhD in Electrical & Computer Engineering, University of Patras (2014). IEEE Senior Member.

I work on post-quantum cryptography and the practical problem of migrating deployed systems to it:
protocol performance, cryptographic hardware, and the standards that decide what "migrated" means.

### Research focus

- **Post-quantum cryptography** — NIST candidate evaluation, lattice-based schemes, post-quantum TLS 1.3, crypto-agility and migration planning
- **Cryptographic hardware** — NTT accelerators for lattice cryptography, VLSI/FPGA, TPM and hardware roots of trust, side-channel and fault-attack resistance
- **Privacy-preserving computation** — fully homomorphic encryption (CKKS), zero-knowledge proofs, privacy-preserving machine learning
- **Network and systems security** — 5G/6G and IoT security architecture, agentic AI security (DID / verifiable credentials)
